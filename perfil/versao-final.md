## 2. Sobre (EN)

I've spent 16+ years in oil & gas — more than 12 of them on FPSOs — helping complex offshore operations run safely without losing sight of production.

Today I'm Safety Officer at Yinson Production on FPSO Maria Quitéria (Petrobras), working as part of the offshore leadership team and supporting the OIM directly. I followed this unit from the start: I was responsible for Pre-Operations HSSE during construction and commissioning in China, sailed with her to Brazil, and directly supported the regulatory and client audits and acceptance — with the operating licenses obtained ahead of schedule. On October 15, 2026, our team reached 2 years since first oil with zero Lost Time Injuries.

Before Yinson, I spent 9 years with SBM Offshore on FPSO Cidade de Anchieta (Petrobras) and FPSO Espírito Santo (Shell).

What I bring to an offshore team:
• Operational leadership — supporting the OIM and connecting Operations, Marine, Maintenance and contractors
• Process safety & operational risk — PTW, SIMOPS, barrier management, HAZID/HAZOP
• Emergency response — drills, contingency planning, calm decisions under pressure
• Commissioning, startup & operational readiness — from shipyard to first oil
• Planned production shutdowns — coordinating HSSE for multiple maintenance shutdowns, successfully executed
• Incident investigation — real root causes and actions that actually get closed
• Compliance — Petrobras requirements, NRs, ANP and ISM Code
• Onboard roles — Radiation Protection Lead Technician and Helicopter Landing Officer (HLO)

I believe good offshore operations are built by people. I like being close to the operation, understanding the real risks and helping different departments work together. I value open communication, trust, ownership and continuous improvement — and I'm not afraid to challenge the status quo when it's needed. My goal is to keep growing as an offshore operations leader.

B.Sc. in Production Engineering, with postgraduate degrees in Occupational Safety Engineering and in Maintenance Engineering & Management. NEBOSH IGC · IOSH Managing Safely · Process Safety Management. Fluent in English.

Based in Guarapari, Brazil — available for offshore assignments in Brazil and worldwide.
